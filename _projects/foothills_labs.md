**Foothills Labs** is an AI foundation lab I am building. Its first two projects share a theme, letting agents do research honestly:

- [labloop](https://github.com/foothills-labs/labloop), an agent-driven experiment loop that proposes a change, runs it time-boxed, and keeps it only if the metric improves.
- [regexbench](https://github.com/foothills-labs/regexbench), an evaluator for LLM-generated regular expressions covering semantic equivalence, correctness, and ReDoS safety.

Follow along on [GitHub](https://github.com/foothills-labs).
