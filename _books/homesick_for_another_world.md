Ottessa Moshfegh's first collection of short stories: fourteen tales of misfits, loners, and quietly desperate people, rendered with her signature deadpan precision and mordant humor. The characters are unlovely and unlucky, yet Moshfegh writes them with such unflinching attention that their small hungers become strangely luminous.
