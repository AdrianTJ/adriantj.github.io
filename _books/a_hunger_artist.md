The last collection Kafka prepared for publication, gathering "A Hunger Artist" alongside "First Sorrow," "A Little Woman," and "Josephine the Singer." The title story follows a professional faster whose art is slowly rendered obsolete, a quietly devastating parable about devotion, misunderstood vocation, and the artist starved of an audience.
